Entry for the [Rename the Planet](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-rename-the-planet-094b0369) bounty on make.fun: one line under 200 characters that gives a planet of our solar system a funny new name and says why in the same breath.

## Entry

> Rename Venus 'The Two-Hour Hotel': every probe that checks in dies within two hours — crushed, melted, and still charged full price.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating that it is 1–200 characters of non-whitespace UTF-8 text. The entry is deterministic: the same line on every run.
